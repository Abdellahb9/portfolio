// About Me

Get to know me

Building complete systems, not isolated exercises.

I'm Abdellah Bedda, a final-year Software Engineering student specializing in AI & Data Analysis at Universiapolis in Laayoune, Morocco, graduating in 2027.

Most of what I've learned came from building complete systems: a MongoDB schema behind a JWT-secured Express API with distinct admin, owner, and technician roles; a React dashboard wired to it with interactive maps and charts; a MediaPipe and dlib pipeline tuned until face recognition ran in real time; and a Flutter game shipped with localization and ad integration.

I care about the parts users actually feel — clear interfaces, sensible defaults, and software that stays usable for people who aren't technical. Longer term I want to continue my studies internationally, in Germany or France, through a master's or double-degree program.
