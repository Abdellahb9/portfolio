01 — About

Engineering across the whole stack

I'm a final-year software engineering student at Universiapolis in Laayoune, Morocco, specializing in artificial intelligence and data analysis, and graduating in 2027.

Most of what I've learned came from building complete systems rather than isolated exercises: a MongoDB schema behind a JWT-secured Express API with distinct admin, owner, and technician roles; a React dashboard wired to it with interactive maps and charts; a MediaPipe and dlib pipeline tuned until face recognition ran in real time; a Flutter game shipped with localization and ad integration.

I care about the parts users actually feel — clear interfaces, sensible defaults, and software that stays usable for people who aren't technical. Longer term I want to continue my studies internationally, in Germany or France, through a master's or double-degree program.
